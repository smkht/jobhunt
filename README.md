# jobhunt

A local, review-first job-search assistant. It finds fresh postings from public sources, scores how well each one fits your background,
drafts an ATS-matched CV and a short cover letter for the best ones, and gives you an apply kit and a tracker. **It never applies, sends or posts anything for you.**

Everything runs on your own computer. Your CV, answers and applications stay in the git-ignored `data/` folder.

## What you need
- Python 3.9 or newer (standard library only, nothing to `pip install`)
- Google Chrome or another Chromium browser (used to turn drafts into PDFs)
- An AI for reading your CV and tailoring documents: the [Claude CLI](https://claude.com/claude-code) logged in on this computer, **or** an Anthropic API key

## Start
```bash
git clone https://github.com/<you>/jobhunt.git
cd jobhunt
python3 app.py
```
Your browser opens at http://127.0.0.1:8765. On the first run it shows a short setup:

1. **Connect an AI** (CLI or API key).
2. **Your CV**: upload a PDF, Word or text file, or paste it. The AI drafts your profile from it. It only uses facts that are in the CV.
3. **Check your profile**: fix anything, remove what you don't want used. This is the only source of facts for your CVs and letters.
4. **What you want**: job titles, remote or local, regions you can take remote jobs from, languages you work in, words to avoid.
5. **Answers and sources**: salary, start date, work authorisation, and which job sites to search.

Then the first search runs and your board opens. Re-run the setup any time at `/setup`.

## Daily use
- **Board**: new roles with a fit score and the requirements you meet or lack. Open one for its apply kit: posting link, tailored CV and letter, form answers to copy, checklist.
- **Draft**: tailors a CV and cover letter for a role from your profile (press the button, or let `daily.py` draft the top matches).
- **Mark applied / interview / rejected** to keep the tracker, funnel and weekly goal honest.
- **+ Add posting**: paste any posting URL or text to get the same kit.

Run a search from the terminal: `python3 search.py`. Search and draft in one go: `python3 daily.py`.
Twice a day on macOS: `./install_schedule.sh`.

## Where things are
| | |
|---|---|
| `data/profile.json` | your facts (see `PROFILE_SCHEMA.md`) |
| `data/config.json` | your search rules, generated by the setup, hand-editable |
| `data/answers.json` | reusable form answers |
| `data/tracker.csv` | every role and its status |
| `data/out/` | drafts, PDFs, notes per application |
| `defaults/config.json` | shared sources and company boards |
| `examples/` | a fictional sample profile and answers |

## Privacy
- Your data never leaves your computer except the text sent to the AI you chose (your profile and the job posting) when drafting.
- The dashboard listens on `127.0.0.1` only. Don't expose it to the internet.
- `data/` and every PDF are git-ignored. Check `git status` before you push a fork.

## Limits
- Works best for English-language postings. Roles that need a language you don't list are ranked down.
- Uses free public sources (LinkedIn public pages, company career boards, remote job boards). Sources change; some searches will find nothing on some days.
- The AI can be wrong. Read every draft before you use it.

## Test without an AI
`TAILOR_MOCK=1 python3 app.py` uses a stand-in for the AI so you can try the whole flow with the sample profile.
